=====<Movelist>=====

 U - up          x - light punch        a - light kick
 D - down        y - medium punch       b - medium kick
 F - forward     z - heavy punch        c - heavy kick
 B - back        p - any punch          k - any kick
 s - start       2p- two punches        2k- two kicks        3k- three kicks 

 (Air) - Move must be performed in the air.
 (EX)  - Move has an EX version, performed by pressing two punch/kick buttons.
 (MAX) - Use two punch/kick buttons when performing a Super move to power it up.

=====<Move Details>=====

 - Hold the P button to charge Bad Stone and increase its damage.
 - Bad Stone get changed for Bad Knife when there is a knife on Cody's hand. The same foes for Fakes.
 - EX Ruffian Kick can slide under projectiles.
 - If done nearby, Cody can slide under falling opponent with EX Ruffian Kick.
 - Bad Spray must be done right before Cody falls on the ground from attack he can recover of.
 - Last Dread Dust is juggle-perfect if connects to falling enemy, but it won't play out full version of super.

=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage


CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing
   faster and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start

=====<Miscellaneous>=====

 - This Cody has 2 SFF Files: "CasualSuitSprite.sff" for FF Outfit and "JailSuitSprite.sff" for SFA3 Outfit.
 To change the outfit just go to Cody.def and change the name at "sprite=...". Both outfits are compatible with
 each other in palette's sctuctire, meaning that you can use the same palettes on both, so you won't have to worry
 about them.
