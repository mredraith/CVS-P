3. Controls

Basic Moves

X, Y, Z, etc, refer to MUGEN button notation.

Light Punch - X
Medium Punch - Y
Heavy Punch - Z
Light Kick - A
Medium Kick - B
Heavy Kick - C


Command Moves & Throws

Strike Arch - Forward+A (If linked from Standing Light/Medium Punch or from Standing Light/Medium Kick, will not cause knockdown)
Leona Crush (throw) - when close to the opponent, press X+Y
Order Buckler (throw) - when close to the opponent, press A+B
Heidern Inferno (throw) - in air, press A+B; works on airborne opponents only


System

Recovery - any two buttons, if recovery is possible
Dodge - while standing, press X+A
	Counter - while dodging, press X or A
Forward Roll - while standing, hold Forward and press X+A
Backwards Roll - while standing, hold Back and press X+A
Power Charge - when standing, hold Y+B
Parry - Tap F (to parry high moves) or D (to parry low). Usable in air.
High Jump - Tap D twice, then jump in any direction.
	Running High Jump - while running, jump forward or backwards.

Special Moves
(To perform an EX move, execute the command and use two attack buttons instead of one. EX moves cost 500 power to use.)



Charge Down, Up, P - Moon Slasher - Leona creates a cutting aura around her. Hits falling opponents.
	*EX version: Leona cuts twice, moving forward each time. Hits falling opponents.
	
Chaarge Back, Forward, P - Baltic Launcher - Leona summons a ball of energy that stays in front of her (X or Y version) or travels forward (Z version). The opponent is 
sent into a fall state when the move is complete.
	*EX version: Leona stays grounded while the ball travels forward; her recovery time is much faster, allowing her to attack freely before the opponent is knocked 
	away, provided she is close enough, and the ball will stop in place when it hits the opponent. Also can juggle falling opponents.

Hold Back, Forward, K - Grand Saber - Leona dashes forward and cuts low at the opponent if they come close enough to her before she stops running. 
Must be blocked low. Can hit falling opponents.
	   Forward+C (after succesful C Grand Saber) - Gliding Buster - Leona flips forward, creating another cutting aura. Can hit falling opponents.
	*EX version: Leona dashes straight to the opponent and chains together a Grand Saber and a Gliding Buster, doing three hits total. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DB, B, P (air) - X-Caliber - Leona creates a slashing projectile in front of her. X version causes her to flip upwards after execution, 
Y version causes her to flip forward after execution, and Z version causes her to flip back. The X version of the projectile is stationary, while the Y
and Z versions travel forward and down.
	*EX version: Leona dive-bombs the opponent, covered in a cutting aura. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DB, B, P (ground) - I-Slasher - Leona rolls back (with increasing distance based on the button) and tosses a razor boomerang in an elliptical arc. 
The X version is the shortest ranged while the Z version is the longest, but recovery time increases with the stronger versions. The boomerang can hit two times in total.
It can be destroyed by projectiles of greater or equal stength.
	*EX version: Leona tosses the boomerang straight forward; it stops at the opponent's position and hits multiple times. Hits falling opponents. 
      It can be destroyed by projectiles of greater or equal stength.

D, DB, B, K - Earring Bomb - Leona takes off her earring and throws it at the opponent. The button used determines the distance that the earring is thrown.
The earring bounces once before exploding harmlessly. Hits once, causes fall.
	*EX version: Heart Attack - Leona quickly takes off her earring and tries attaching it to the opponent. If successful, the earring will explode after 3 seconds,
	or if the command is input again. However, the earring will disappear if Leona grabs the opponent or uses any super moves. 
	

Super Moves 
Lv. 1 supers require 1000 or more power (1 stock).
Lv. 2s require 2000 or more power (2 stocks).
Lv. 3/MAX requires 3000 power (3 stocks).

Lv. 1

D, DF, F, D, B, P (air only) - V-Slasher - Leona flies at the opponent with her hand extended for a chop. If she hits the opponent, she slashes downwards, then 
upwards, summoning a MAZINGER TRIBUTE V to blow them away as she leaps up. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DB, B, D, F, K - Rebel Spark - Leona dashes forward and attempts to plant dynamite on the opponent. If she's successful, a KAMEN RIDER TRIBUTE COMMENCES
followed by an explosion. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DF, F, D, DF, F, P - Gravity Storm - Leona kicks twice, knocking the opponent up into the air, then stabs them in the back, blowing them up after removing
her hand and dropping to the ground. Can hit falling opponents.


Lv. 2

D, DF, F, D, B, 2P (air only) - V-Slasher - Leona flies at the opponent with her hand extended for a chop. If she hits the opponent, she slashes downwards, 
then upwards, summoning an even larger MAZINGER TRIBUTE V to blow them away as she leaps up. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DB, B, D, F, 2K - Rebel Spark - Leona is overtaken by her Orochi power, wildly dashes forward and attempts to plant dynamite on the opponent. 
If she's successful, a KAMEN RIDER TRIBUTE COMMENCES followed by a wild scream from her and an explosion. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DF, F, D, DF, F, 2P - Gravity Storm -  Leona kicks four times, knocking the opponent up into the air, then stabs them in the back, 
blowing them up after removing her hand and dropping to the ground. Can hit falling opponents.

D, DF, F, D, DF, F, 2K - Rider Burst -  Leona dashes forward for a short distance. If the opponent comes close to her, she performs a Grand Saber, then 
launches into an auto-combo that ends with a Leona Crush. Once the dynamite planted on the opponent goes out, Leona rolls back and performs a flying kick to
engulf the opponent in a fiery explosion. Can hit falling opponents.


MAX

D, DF, F, D, DF, F, Z+C (ground or air) - V-Slasher: Revision LDM - Leona rolls back, then launches forward with a leaping kick. If successful in
hitting the opponent, she executes two more attacks, then flips up higher into the air and drops down with a much more painful V-Slasher. 
The air version is a dive-bomb similar to the EX X-Calibur (with a much smaller angle), but does the same as the ground version upon connecting successfully. 
Can hit falling opponents.


4. System Notes

Chaining/Comboing
All of these links assume that the moves either hit or make contact. Whiffed moves do not allow for combos or chains.

Light Basics (Standing) -------> Medium OR Heavy Basics of same type (Standing) 
	(e.g., Standing Light Punch-->Standing Medium or Heavy Punch)
Standing Light Punch --> Standing Light Punch or Crouching Light Punch
Standing Light Kick --> Crouching Light Kick
Standing Light/Medium Punch, Stand Light Kick, Close Medium Kick  -------> Strike Arch
Standing/Crouching Basics (except for Standing Strong Punch/Kick, Crouching Heavy Kick) -------> All Ground Specials
Crouching Light Punch --> Crouching Light Punch
All Air Basics -----> X-Calibur
All Standing/Crouching Basics -----> Rebel Spark, Gravity Storm, Grateful Dead, Rider Burst
Strike Arch, Air Basics -------> V-Slasher, V-Slasher: Revision L.D.M.


Some special moves can be Super Canceled into regular supers. The power cost still applies.
	Grand Saber (not EX, without Gliding Buster follow-up)-->Gravity Storm (any level) OR Rebel Spark (any level)
	X-Calibur (not EX, projectile must make contact)-->V-Slasher (any level)
	
Additionally, some Super Moves can be Dream Canceled into other supers. The 2000 power cost still applies.
	Rebel Spark (before Leona removes her hand)-->Rider Burst

Note that with each successive cancel, damage does drop.

5. Bonus Section

Intro information
(Quote information only applies to the normal "cvszeroleona" and "cvszeroleona-mi2j" soundpacks.)

Intro 1: Leona takes her dog tags from around her neck and uses them to tie her ponytail up.
Intro 2 (against Heidern only): Leona straightens her uniform and salutes, saying, "Leona Heidern, kanarazu ninmu suikou shimasu!" ([I], Leona Heidern, will
definitely/absolutely accomplish my mission!)
Intro 3: Leona, dressed in formal wear, speaks into her radio watch, saying,"Ninmu, suikou shimasu." (I will accomplish my mission.)
	 She then jumps up and throws away the clothes, revealing her regular outfit.
Intro 4 (against herself, or at random): Leona shakes her head and says, "Anata de wa katenai." (You cannot win.)
Intro 5 (against Chang, Ralf, and Clark only): Leona salutes, and then takes her stance.
Intro 6 (against Cammy, Guile, and Nash): Leona adjusts her shoelaces, then looks up, stands, and asks, "O-matase?" (Did I keep you waiting?)

Winpose information

Winpose 1 (against Cammy, Guile, and Nash, or at random): Leona opens up her jacket, straightens it, and closes it. She then crosses her arms and looks to 
the opponent, saying, "Anata. Watashi no na o itemite." (You. Say my name.)
Winpose 2 (with a perfect victory in either match, or at random): Leona slashes her arm out, then clasps her hands together in front of her face, 
turned towards the camera.
Winpose 3: Leona bends down to adjust her laces, and looks up at her fallen opponent. After a short time, she returns to adjusting her laces.
Winpose 4: Leona pulls her glove down to reveal her radio watch, and says, "Ninmu, kanryou." (Mission complete.) Shortly afterwards, she pulls her hair out
	   of its ponytail.
Winpose 5: Leona turns towards the opponent, puts a hand on her hip, and says, "Ninmu, kanryou."
Winpose 6 (against herself, or at random): Leona straightens one of her gloves while her hair changes colors. As it returns to normal, she says, "Chikara wa seigyo 
	    dekiru kedo, sore ni tayoru tsumori wa nai." (I can control my power, but I have no intention of using it.)
Winpose 7: Leona shakes her head and says, "Anata de wa katenai." (You cannot win.) 
Winpose 8 (against Ralf, Clark, and Heidern): Leona salutes.
