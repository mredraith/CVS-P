---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GENERAL OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Urban Champion is a slow brawler that sucks ass at long range but excels in footsies and
close range. For those who haven't played him in Famicom Fighters, think of him as Dudley stuck
in molasses.

He's got great pokes such as st.MP, cr.MK, and st.HK, with the former 2 able to reliably 
cancel into his super, Urban Beatdown. His close range mixup is scary with his command dash, 
Quick Dash, having 3 followups that hit mid, low, and overhead respectively, allowing him to 
enforce scary mixups during his blockstrings. His main TC, Punch Pak, allows him to hit-confirm
into MP Fists of Fury, setting up a hard knockdown that he can pressure with normals after.
His defense is solid as well for a brawler, having his DP, Rising Champion, and his command dodge,
Counter Attack to rely on.

He struggles in long range due to his movement attributes being extremely ass, with a horrid
combination of both a bad walk speed and a slow, floaty jump. He often has to force his way in
by guessing with Quick Dash, and while MK and HK versions have high/mid invuln on startup, they're
a bit unreliable as fireball punishes. Don't think about rolling either, as his roll is both slower
and longer than average. Lastly, he's a surprisingly tall croucher for a character of his size,
meaning that he has to deal with attacks head-on that most other characters could crouch under.

2010 Mode trades his defense and anti-air prowess for a more bully-oriented playstyle. He gains
Ultimate Haymaker, a Dash Punch styled attack, and Street Smasher, a 2-hit rekka that he can use
as approach options, the former being safe on block and leaves the opponent close. When he gets in
the opponent's face, his mixup is more Strike/Throw oriented, using his various strikes, and his new
command grab, Downtown Beatdown, to open up his opponents. If he's feeling cheeky, he can go for his
overhead special, Flowerpot Drop, to remind the opponent to block high, or as a high risk fireball
callout.

Sadly, his TCs are not as good in utility, as Low Smash has much worse range than Punch Pak, and
Face Breaker is only situationly useful due to the distance it knocks the opponent away. His defense
is worse as well, as he has to spend meter on EX Street Smasher or EX Downtown Beatdown, which both
are finicky against jump-ins. His anti-airs, while servicable, are not as threatening as Normal Mode's 
DP, which combined with 2010 Mode's more effective ground game, can cause him to deal with swatting 
the opponent's jumps more often.

Boss Mode is well, boss mode. Takes a bunch of creative liberties with the visuals that may or may
not be annoying, and combines both Normal and 2010 mode. Takes more damage if a human pilots him,
but if it's an AI? Well, good luck >_>

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMAND NORMALS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5LP~5MP - Punch Pak
A jab into a straight punch, and your new best friend. Punch Pak is Urban Champ's main Target Combo
used during both his combos and his blockstrings, as 5MP is special cancellable, unlike when it's
performed raw.

5LK~5MK - Clu Clu Clock
An uppercut, into a gut punch, into another uppercut that launches. Cancellable into a High Jump
during the second hit of 5MK, which lets Urban Champ get some cheeky air resets. Best used as a
punish, as 5LK is stubby and the 5MK followup is -7 on block and leaves the opponent kinda close.
High Jump Cancelling on block is ill-advised with Urban Champ's slow and floaty jump, unless
you're a parry god or whatever.

2LP~2MP - Balloon Buster
A crouch jab into an upwards punch, for when 5LP whiffs on a crouching opponent, disabling you
to mindlessly use Punch Pak. Damage is better, but has some issues connecting at 2LP's max range,
so be careful.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIAL MOVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

236K - Quick Dash
A command dash that acts as Urban Champ's main way of closing the distance. MK has high/mid invuln
on startup, HK has high/mid invuln throughout the entire dash. Use the MK/HK versions to dodge pokes
in neutral, while using the LK version during blockstrings/combos.

Has 3 followups after the initial dash, all of which are super cancellable:

	LK - Face Breaker
	A face punch that leaves Urban Champ +2. The safest followup that unfortunately whiffs on
	crouchers. Against foes who can't crouch under it, abuse it to your heart's content.
	
	MK - Ankle Sweeper
	A low punch aimed at the opponent's ankles. Effectively an upgraded version of his sweep, and
	can be spaced to be safe on block. Has enough time midscreen to dash in and go for another mixup
	if he lands this.
	
	HK - Head Smasher
	An overhead punch that leaves Urban Champ -2. As the name implies, it's an overhead, and a safe
	one too. Since Face Breaker whiffs on most crouchers, this is your main reminder for the opponent
	to respect your mix.

EX Quick Dash is fast and covers the same distance as the MK version, and it has enhanced followups.
Face Breaker becomes a launcher, Ankle Sweeper can be linked out of on hit, and Head Smasher inflicts
a Hard Knockdown on hit.

623P - Rising Champion
Standard multihit uppercuts. Good anti-airs/reversals, very punishable on block. EX Rising Champion
gets more invincibility and better frame advantage to work with to work with, but worse on block.

63214P - Fists of Fury
A barrage of punches with an ender. LP ends with a punch to the face, MP ends with a punch that
inflicts a Hard Knockdown, HP ends with a punch that acts as a launcher. Strength determines the
distance traveled when Urban Champ performs his first swing. All versions are death on block, so
they're usually reserved as his meterless combo enders. EX Fists of Fury has the speed of LP,
distance traveled of HP, and Hard Knockdown of MP.

5KKK - Counter Attack
Urban Champ dodges any incoming attack and retaliates with a punch that knocks down. Urban Champ's
other meterless reversal, slow, but safe. Fully invincible on startup 
unlike the POTS dodge, allowing him to bait throw attempts.

Has 2 followups he can go into before he swings:

	44 - Duck and Weave
	Urban Champ slides backwards, then retaliates with a gut punch. Throw invincible on the slide
	backwards, acting as an auto-shimmy. Leaves him 0 on contact, which is the safest out of all 
	3 of his command dodge attacks, but doesn't inflict a knockdown.
	
	66 - Power Punch
	Urban Champ does a stupid little spin punch. Has high/mid intangibility during startup, which
	allows it to be an anti-fireball tool or as a huge callout against an active poke. 

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPER MOVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

236236P - Urban Beatdown
A dash into a barrage of punches. Acts as his main combo ender.

2141236P - Flowerpot Crash
Urban Champ pulls out a flowerpot and leaps forward, cracking it on the opponent's head. Distance
for the Level 1 version is determined by the punch used, Level 2 version is steerable. This super
is Urban Champ's fireball callout, as it has fireball invincibility until he lands. He can combo
after both versions, the Level 1 being a crumple, the Level 2 being a stun.

214214K - Scapegoat
Urban Champ looks around like an idiot waiting to get his ass kicked. If the opponent hits him,
Urban Champ takes a step back to avoid a police car, which rams into the opponent. Risky reversal
super that's more funny than it is useful. Could be used as a hard callout against big pokes.
No Level 2 Version.

236236KK - Sewer Brawl
Urban Champ knocks the opponent into the sewers with a big uppercut, and then follows them inside
to kick their ass. A fast, safe, very invincible super that he can use to surprise people in 
neutral, or as a flashy combo finisher.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2010 COMMAND NORMALS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6MP - Head Crusher
His overhead punch from his Normal Mode's command dash. 0 on contact and super cancels, a decent
overhead he can't cancel into.

5LP~5HP - Face Breaker
A jab into a high punch that knocks the opponent away. Face Breaker is only useful in the corner
due to how far the opponent is knocked away on hit. -6 on block with lots of pushback makes it
decent as a blockstring ender, though Urban Champ can only really threaten HP/EX Street Smasher
afterwards from that range.

5LK~5HK - Gut Smasher
An uppercut into a gut punch. Essentially, "We have Punch Pak at home". 5LK being really stubby
makes it much worse in utility compared to Normal Urban Champ's Punch Pak, but in exchange, the
damage is much higher, and ending with HK gives it more hitstun to work with. While you'll land it
less in neutral, it'll hurt a lot more than Punch Pak.

2LP~2HP - Balloon Brawler
A crouching jab into an uppercut. Much worse on contact if Urban Champ doesn't cancel into anything
compared to Balloon Buster, but again, he gets more hitstun to work with since it ends on a heavy.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2010 SPECIAL MOVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

214K - Street Smasher
Urban Champion advances forward with a punch. A two-part rekka. Strength determines the distance 
travelled, though you'll usually stick to the HK version. His main approach option in neutral if
he doesn't have charge, but unfortunately whiffs on crouchers.

	214K - Street Upper
	The second part of the rekka that knocks down. Delay it to mess with the opponent.
	
EX Street Smasher has 1 hit of armor before he swings, and he can juggle after EX Street Upper.

[4]6K - Ultimate Haymaker
Urban Champion advances forward... but with like... a stronger punch. Leaves Urban Champ safe and
in the opponent's face, like Balrog's Dash Punch if it traded range for frame advantage. 
Much stronger than his rekka, but unfortunately Ultimate Haymaker is charge so he'll have to find 
ways to build it. EX version wall bounces but is super unsafe on block.

63214P - Downtown Beatdown
Urban Champ grabs the opponent and beats their ass. The command grab's a bit slow by CvS standards,
but with the added threat of his rekkas and dash punch, the opponent will be opened up somehow.
The EX version has invincibility on startup, acting as a reversal.

623P - Flowerpot Drop
Urban Champ pulls out a flowerpot and leaps forward, cracking it on the opponent's head. Distance
is determined by the punch used. An overhead that causes a Hard Knockdown on hit, and is relatively 
safe if blocked. HP version is a gimmicky fireball callout. EX Flowerpot drop only goes as far as the
LP version, but crumples on hit.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2010 SUPER MOVES
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

214214P - Street Fight
Urban Champ performs a street fight, but with none of those magic wizard moves. Decent combo ender.

214214K - Alley Uppercut
Urban Champ uppercuts the opponent. Level 1 version is a dash into an uppercut, Level 2 is a 
Shoryureppa-style super. Use this super if you're not confident that Street Fight can pick up your
opponent from farther ranges.

222PP - Playing with Power
Urban Champ flexes like an idiot and embarasses himself so bad he turns purple. An install super
that does two things:
	- Drops the charge time for Ultimate Haymaker.
	- All of his normals become special/super cancellable.
These two things make him *significantly* more threatening. Now any stray hit can convert into a
special move, and he can use his Dash Punches with reckless abandon. Thankfully, he needs 2 meters
to activate this MAX only super.

2363214PP - Raging Champion
Urban Champion beats the opponent's ass in a very extended fashion. Not a Raging Demon parody.
5th attack hits low so it can catch people offguard, but other than that, it's a typical Level 3.
