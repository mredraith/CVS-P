------------------------------------I.  Character Origins-----------------------------------------
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

It's the guy from Urban Champion for the NES. Everyone wanted a POTS-styled version of him, right?

What started out as a funny concept (taking the Urban Champion guy and converting him to POTStile)
somehow blossomed into *whatever this is*. He's primarily based off of the Famicom Fighters
version of Urban Champ, though there have been several liberties taken to more closely match the
CvS2-esque style.

He also comes bundled with 2010 Mode, an EX Mode based off of KingPepe's really old version of
Urban Champ created back in 2010, and Boss Mode, a mixture of the 2 modes with some cheeky new FX.

Link to the original character below:
https://mcjimmy.net/famicomfighters.html

As for the link to the really old 2010 Urban Champ, find it yourself. ;P

=====<Move Details>=====

 - Punch Pak and Balloon Buster (Normal) can be special cancelled.
 - Clu Clu Clock (Normal) launches on the final hit and can be cancelled into Super Jump.
 - Head Crusher (2010) hits as an overhead. 
 - Gut Smasher and Balloon Brawler (2010) can be special cancelled.
 - Medium Quick Dash (Normal) has upper body invincibility on startup.
 - Heavy Quick Dash (Normal) has upper body invincibility during the entire move.
 - Ankle Sweeper (Normal) hits low, EX version has enough plus frames on hit to link out of.
 - Head Smasher (Normal) hits as an overhead, EX version causes a hard knockdown.
 - EX Face Breaker (Normal) launches for a juggle reset.
 - Rising Champion and Counter Attack (Normal) have full body invincibility on startup.
 - Medium and EX Fists of Fury (Normal) causes a hard knockdown.
 - Heavy Fists of Fury (Normal) launches for a juggle reset.
 - EX Street Smasher (2010) has one hit of armor.
 - EX Street Upper (2010) launches for a juggle reset.
 - Flowerpot Drop (2010) hits as an overhead and causes a hard knockdown. EX causes a crumple.
 - EX Ultimate Haymaker (2010) wall bounces for a juggle reset.
 - Downtown Beatdown (2010) is a command grab that causes a hard knockdown.
 - EX Downtown Beatdown (2010) has full body invincibility.
 - Flowerpot Crash (Normal) hits as an overhead and causes a crumple. MAX causes a stun.
 - Scapegoat (Normal) is a counter super.
 
 PLAYING WITH POWER:
 - Lasts 7.5 seconds.
 - All grounded normals becomes special/super cancellable.
 - Drops the charge time required for Ultimate Haymaker.
 - You can't build meter while the install is active.
 
 BOSS MODE:
 - Mixes both Normal and 2010 Mode together.
 - Command for Flowerpot Drop changed to D, DF, F, p.
 - Command for Scapegoat changed to F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, k.
 - F, DF, D, DB, B, p is Fists of Fury. F, DF, D, DB, B, 2p is EX Downtown Beatdown.
 - Contains various cosmetic differences.
 
=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start

------------------------------------III.  Configuration Notes-------------------------------------
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open up the "_CONFIG.txt" file with whatever text editor you like! You're able to change variables
that affect Urban Champion's gameplay, which are gone into more detail here.

Mode Settings - Changes the mode Urban Champion plays as during the match.
	0 (Normal Mode)
	Default on Palettes 1-6. Heavily based off the Famicom Fighters version of Urban Champion.
	
	1 (2010 Mode)
	Default on Palettes 7-12. Loosely based off KingPepe's old Urban Champion from 2010, and the
	2010 Mode of Famicom Fighters Urban Champion.
	
	2 (Boss Mode)
	A fusion of Normal and 2010 Mode, with cheeky cosmetic changes. If piloted by a player, he
	takes more damage due to being a boss character.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Urban Champion has special intros against himself, pretty women, and Mega Man by NovaMagnolia.

Urban Champion has some debugging features I left in that activate in Training Mode, which are:
	- Double tapping Start to cycle between Modes.
	- Being able to Power Charge even with full meter.

When using Scapegoat, a different vehicle has a tiny chance of appearing when the super activates.
The chances rise to 50% if Urban Champion is in Boss Mode.
