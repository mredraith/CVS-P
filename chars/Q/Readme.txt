=====<Move Details>=====

 - Taunt increases Q's defense up to three times.
 - Standing Light Kick hits low.
 - Dashing Head Attack is a overhead attack.
 - Dashing Leg Attack is a low attack.
 - You can juggle after Capture & Deadly Blow.
 - EX Capture & Deadly Blow has startup invincibility. 
 - You can juggle after MAX Deadly Double Combination.
 - Automated Mauling is a reversal super.

TOTAL DESTRUCTION:
 - Lasts 8 seconds.
 - You get access to Total Destruction: Activate and Total Destruction: Danger.
 - Total Destruction: Activate is a hitgrab that can be blocked.
 - Total Destruction: Danger is a command grab that can't be blocked.
 - You can't use Power Charge, Zero Counter, Custom Combo, EX Specials, or Supers while the buff is active.


=====<Gameplay Notes>=====

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Some Normal attacks can be canceled into Command, Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Some Lv1 Super moves can be canceled into MAX Super moves
 - Cancelling a Special, Super move or Custom Combo into a Super move resets the juggle points
   but also reduces its damage

CUSTOM COMBO:
 - Removes cancellable attack restrictions and gives you a lot of freedom to combo them
 - Juggle limit is lifted
 - You can only use EX Special or Super moves from the point the character starts flashing faster
   and brighter, and doing so ends Custom Combo
 - Gives you a short invulnerability window at the start
