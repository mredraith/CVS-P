NES Megaman Edit by Rbade (COMPO 8-Bit Mega Man)
---------------------------------------
	This is classic Mega Man. He can change weapons by going into the start menu. His controls are flipped from the original build. 

  Controls
  ---------------------------------------

    Shoot               - . (b)
    Jump                - , (a)
    Menu                - enter (start)
