(**Note: 1-10 Ranking System: 1 = Lowest, 10 = Best)

 - Intelligence: 7
 - Strength: 5
 - Speed: 8
 - Stamina: 5
 - Energy Projection: 2
 - Fighting Ability: 7

One of 12 lovely, yet deadly, "dolls" genetically enhanced(created...?) by M.Bison(Vega), Juli.
Essentially just like Cammy, however she has some extra unique traits that sets her apart from Cammy.
Overall though, you'd handle Juli the same as you would Cammy. Her high speed is key.

----------------<Move Details>-------------------

 - Falling Arc is a overhead
 - Flying Neck Hunt and Air Frankensteiner can catch foes in their hit states and isn't affected by the juggle system
 - (EX)Cannon Spike has invulnerability on start up
 - (EX)Cannon Strike can only be performed at the top of a jump during high/normal jumps, it also OTGs
 - (EX)Spin Knuckle resists normal/special projectiles and has mid body invulnerability
 - EX Spin Knuckle bounces foes off walls, allowing Juli to follow up with attacks
 - (EX)Earth Direct is a grab and has high priority
 - Mach Slide distance covered depends on what strength punch used to perform it
 - Mach Slide has upper body invulnerability on start up and complete invulnerability as Juli dashes
 - Mash buttons during Reverse Shaft Breaker(MAX) to add more hits/damage
 - Psycho Whirl is a overhead
 - The first hit of Death Cross Dancing MUST hit in order for the rest of the move to ensue 
   
---------------<Gameplay Notes>------------------

COMBO SYSTEM:
 - Most Normal standing/crouching attacks can be canceled into Special and Super moves
 - Some Special moves can be canceled into Super moves
 - Performing a Super/MAX Cancel resets the juggle points but also reduces damage dealt
   
MAX MODE:
 - Your attack and defense is increased while in MAX Mode
 - EX moves can be performed at no power cost, though take away from time sustained in MAX mode
 - All Lv1 supers can be performed at no power cost, though immediately uses up all time in MAX mode
 - All Lv2 and higher supers will cost 1 power bar, along with using up all time in MAX mode
 - Even without a power bar, as long as you have time left in MAX mode, you can perform Lv1 supers/EX moves
 - You can not Power Charge/gain power while in MAX mode
 - You can not perform a Alpha Counter/Counter Movement while in MAX Mode
